If you need to say something, these are the clearest ways to reach me.

Send it to the right place

A little context makes a message much easier to answer.

Email is best for website issues, writing, copyright, broken links, or a specific project. Write to itsnhanaz@gmail.com and name the page, repository, or error you mean. One link and a few useful lines usually beat a lone “hello”.

If you are in Vietnam and want a quick message, use Zalo. Telegram is better for something more private. My Discord is NhanAZ, although messages there can disappear into the scroll when they arrive without context.

For code and issues that can be public, an issue or discussion in NhanAZ on GitHub gives other people a chance to inspect the same history. I cannot promise an immediate reply, I do not accept login details, and I will not ask for secrets, tokens, or private data to understand an ordinary bug.

This is not a support desk for Mojang, Microsoft, OpenAI, GitHub, or any other service mentioned in the writing. For a third-party product, check its official documentation first and treat my pages as personal experience or analysis.
