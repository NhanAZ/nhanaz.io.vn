To Those Who Are Laughing...

A poem about laughter beneath peaceful days, the things left unsaid, and a question that stays.

← All writing

How come...
How is everyone so happy?

People talk about peaceful days.
Beneath it all,
laughter follows laughter,
lively as a festival.

Strange...
With peace all around,
why is everyone laughing?

Could they be that happy?
Or is there something else?

I don't know.

I want to ask,
just one question:

“What is there to laugh about?”

Laughter passes by.
The question doesn't.

Luckily...
There is still a place
for laughter.

I look at their laughter,
then wonder:

How many people
have wanted to ask the same question as me?

How many things left unsaid
have been tucked into a laugh?

I don't know.

And I don't know
what those laughs mean either.

Is it real joy?
Or is it all too funny?
Or are there things
that are easier left unsaid?

Strange...
There are people who know for certain
even what others haven't said.

Just one laugh,
and people can understand
so much!

I look again.

Those peaceful days are still there.
Laughter keeps following laughter,
without end.

Everything seems
perfectly peaceful.

And I,
after a stretch of silence,
reach out too
and touch a laugh.

And so,
beneath those peaceful days,
there is one more person

Counted as laughing.

- Nguyễn Thành Nhân - 13 November 2026
